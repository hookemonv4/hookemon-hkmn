# Phase 3 Failure Modes

- Requirements revision: `65`
- Architecture revision: `9`

Each entry is a concrete question that tests, evidence, or recovery procedures must answer before the affected path advances.

| ID | Module | On-call question | Required recovery or evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FM-P3-01 | `deployment-manifest` | Did an excluded former custody component, a proxy, an unbound custody address, or an unauthorized runtime enter the deployment graph? | Reject the graph by content hash before launch; retain the frozen source record without deploying it. |
| FM-OW-01 | `operations-wallet` | Did a caller other than current Operations claim, use a zero or reused cycle ID, redirect the process share, or exceed liability or capacity? | Revert without a state change. Require the event and post-claim capacity to match the exact successful transfer. |
| FM-OW-02 | `operations-wallet` | Did a claim at exactly 21,600 seconds remain counted, did retention exceed `N`, or did an expired record make an identifier reusable? | Expire equality, evict only capacity entries, and preserve permanent cycle-ID history. |
| FM-OW-03 | `operations-wallet` | Did a limit increase take effect early, bypass the immutable 500000 USDG `Xmax`, exceed the hard 64-entry cap, reset usage, or did a rotation clear history, allow Operations cancellation, or leave claims active? | Apply reductions or zero immediately; delay permitted increases for six hours; use the immutable 43200-second rotation delay; allow only a new Treasury intent to replace a pending rotation; auto-pause schedule and rotation; retain all history. |
| FM-OW-04 | `role-control` | Did a Programmable or Treasury claim use a zero destination, pay an address other than the supplied destination, or emit a different destination? | Revert zero destinations and bind transfer plus event to the actual destination. |
| FM-LA-01 | `launch-orchestration` | Did an external caller initialize the pool, did initialization happen outside the launch leg, or did a second launch succeed? | Revert external and repeat attempts. Require hook-self context, launch authority, and one-use stamp. |
| FM-LA-02 | `launch-orchestration` | Did the provider graph omit a target, allocation, custody configuration, fixed-price initialization, or launch stamp; did the separate owner seed leave an allowance, bind a guessed identifier, strand assets, transfer pre-seed HKMN, or enable a swap without liquidity? | Revert the atomic graph on any graph failure. Keep hook and custody HKMN non-transferable and swaps unavailable until the atomic seed succeeds. Set and clear the seed allowance, inspect the actual minted position, send refund and dust to Treasury, and roll back only seed-local state on seed failure so it may retry. |
| FM-CM-01 | `canonical-market` | Did an unsupported pool, self-call same-pool swap, malformed callback, partial fill, or wrong direction mutate accounting? | Authenticate the canonical pool and callback, enforce `same-pool-swap-forbidden`, and use `REVERT_WHOLE_SWAP`. |
| FM-FA-01 | `fee-accounting` | Did any of the eight ordering, direction, and exactness cases use net rather than gross quote volume, or did exact output fail the monotone gross-up equation? | Prove gross quote volume and `gross - sum(fees) = requestedNet` with the minimal valid gross amount. |
| FM-FA-02 | `fee-accounting` | Did a claim reset a 10, 40, or 250 basis-point lifetime remainder, or did a gross amount below 1,000 atomic USDG enter fee accounting? | Maintain three independent accumulators across claims and revert the sub-threshold fee-bearing swap. |
| FM-CR-01 | `cycle-repository` | Did the dashboard, scheduler, or an adapter mutate money without the single repository, or did a restart prepare a replacement action? | Require repository compare-and-swap plus fencing; rebroadcast identical bytes or reconcile the unresolved action. |
| FM-CR-02 | `cycle-repository` | Did a provider mutation or chain transaction cross a journal boundary without a durable write-ahead state, retry after provider contact, or rebroadcast without rechecking its approval digest? | Persist `PREPARED` before the effect; record `NOT_SENT` only for a pre-call failure, treat `SENT_UNKNOWN` as observation-only, and use the full chain-attempt record through `SIGNED`, `BROADCAST`, `FINALIZED`, or fenced `REFUSED`. |
| FM-CR-03 | `cycle-repository` | Did a Relay leg settle from provider status, lack one finalized delta, reuse a source or destination hash, misattribute a destination credit, or use another cycle's funds? | Persist `RelayLegV1`; observe both finalities through own RPC clients; match destination amount, time window, and memo or request identifier; enforce global hash uniqueness; use only claimed principal outbound and only attributed proceeds on return; send partial, refund, late, and wrong-asset outcomes to named `HELD_RELAY_*` terminal states. |
| FM-CR-04 | `cycle-repository` | Did first use accept expired standing authority, persist a decision without reserving cap and nonce, or reserve twice after replay? | Check wall-clock expiry before any signable action and atomically persist `authorityDigest`, `verifiedAt`, `intentDigest`, `dayCapReservation`, and `nonceReservation`; replay is idempotent. |
| FM-TP-01 | `transaction-policy` | Did an opaque EVM or Solana message reach a signer, did its recipient, program, amount, fee payer, deadline, instruction set, or wire schema differ from policy, or did a money minimum or gas cap fall back to an implicit or placeholder amount? | Use the runner-authoritative `hookemon.transaction-policy.v1` version 1 schema, require adapter parity, positively allowlist, require typed money minima and all four gas caps, reject atomic amount `1` as configuration, and revalidate the signed message immediately before broadcast. |
| FM-PE-01 | `policy-engine` | Did an action bypass a spend, cycle, loss, custody, pause, kill-switch, canary, or first-cycle manual-approval control? | Evaluate controls before every signature and retain the decision digest with the journaled action. |
| FM-RUN-01 | `cycle-runner` | Did a stage advance out of `eligibility-snapshot`, `claim-process`, `outbound`, `purchase`, `open`, `epic-gate`, `buyback`, `return`, or `payout` order, or cross production and rehearsal modes? | Reject the transition and reconcile the prior durable state; persist mode at cycle creation and refuse the other mode. |
| FM-RUN-02 | `cycle-runner` | Did another cycle's funds, an unattributed deposit, an unfinalized observation, a duplicated unresolved principal, a non-USDG return, or proceeds outside the cycle's claimed-principal and attributed-proceeds boundaries fund payout? | Keep finalized observed balances distinct from obligations, count unresolved principal once, keep custody ledgers cycle-scoped, pause new claims for unattributed funds, spend only claimed principal outbound, and accept only a finalized attributed USDG credit on return. |
| FM-EG-01 | `policy-engine` | Did equality to the insured threshold hold instead of sell, did a documented Collector field fail validation, or did an unproven insured-value unit trigger an automatic sale? | Sell at equality; accept tiers 1 through 4, integer or numeric-string `contains`, and documented idempotent open only; send missing, invalid, changed, conflicting, or unreconciled data to a fail-closed `HELD_*` state. |
| FM-EG-02 | `policy-engine` | Did a held card auto-sell, or did new claims proceed while the v1 held-card rule applies? | Raise a deduplicated alert, require an idempotent follow-up decision, and block new claims. |
| FM-ES-01 | `eligibility-snapshot` | Was the snapshot taken after claim, against a reorged hash, with incomplete transfer logs, or without mint-minus-burn reconciliation? | Freeze before claim, pin and recheck the finalized hash, replay logs from deployment, and prove completeness with a second source or archive RPC. |
| FM-DP-01 | `direct-payout` | Did an infeasible recipient set begin a claim, did manifest bytes change after first broadcast, or did a frozen recipient disappear from accounting? | Measure feasibility before claim; lock the manifest at first broadcast; record the recipient as a quarantine liability. |
| FM-DP-02 | `direct-payout` | Did a restart lose a `SIGNED` record, a dropped broadcast create a new nonce or calldata, a frozen recipient block the manifest, dust lose cycle provenance, or a recipient payment become final without a finalized exact transfer? | Hold one repository-backed wallet nonce lock across claim, outbound, return, and payout; revalidate the persisted approval digest; search receipts or replace only with the same nonce and calldata; quarantine a frozen recipient without blocking others; atomically persist and consume provenance-bound dust carry; mark final only from durable finality evidence. |
| FM-RE-01 | `release-evidence` | Was code readiness presented as live readiness, or did `launchEligible` omit current provider, manifest, signer, or canary evidence? | Keep `repairMergeEligible` separate and require all current operational facts for `launchEligible`. |
| FM-FEE-01 | `provider-binding` | Did the fee recipient, rate, gross basis, rounding, accrual, or claim mechanism remain unresolved while fee- or graph-dependent work advanced? | Keep FEE-01 open, use plan-default Option A only as a proposed policy, and obtain provider-bound confirmation before dependent work. |
