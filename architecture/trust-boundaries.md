# Phase 3 Trust Boundaries

- Requirements revision: `65`
- Architecture revision: `9`

Each boundary states what crosses it, the nearby abuse case, and the invariant that preserves the money path.

| ID and boundary | Intended use | Adjacent abuse case | Required invariant and trace |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TB-P3-01 owner-decision-to-proposed-architecture | The recorded plan selects the Operations-wallet design and Phase 3 scope. | A plan, draft artifact, code gate, or readiness signal is treated as permission to act externally. | Proposed architecture never grants deployment, signing, broadcast, spending, or publication authority. |
| TB-P3-02 deployment-manifest-to-runtime | A content-addressed manifest names the immutable token, hook, custody, and allowed offchain control set. | A former vault-family component, proxy, generic route runtime, or unbound custody address appears in the release. | The manifest rejects excluded runtime surfaces and fixes the exact graph before launch evidence. |
| TB-P3-03 PoolManager-to-hook-initialization | The bound PoolManager reaches `beforeInitialize` only through the authorized hook-self launch leg. | An external initializer, a later reinitialization, or a foreign pool enters hook state. | Mask `0x20CC`, hook-self context, launch authority, and one-use launch stamp all hold. |
| TB-P3-04 provider-graph-and-owner-seed-to-permanent-custody | The atomic provider graph deploys token, custody, and hook; allocates the complete HKMN supply through `token.allocate(hook)`; configures custody; initializes the fixed-price pool; and stamps launch. The separate owner-signed atomic seed uses at most 240 USDG through Permit2, `MINT_POSITION`, and `SETTLE_PAIR` to custody. | A graph omits a required step, a pre-seed HKMN transfer, a swap occurs without liquidity, a persistent allowance, substituted position ID, incorrect pool or ticks, withdrawal, approval, operator, an HKMN residual, or a seed failure rolls back graph state. | Any graph failure reverts the graph. The complete HKMN allocation remains non-transferable at the hook and swaps are unavailable until seed success. The seed sets and clears allowance, binds the actual mint, returns unused USDG to the payer, reverts on any HKMN residual, and rolls back only seed-local state on failure so it may retry. |
| TB-P3-05 canonical-callback-to-fee-ledger | The canonical market callback supplies gross USDG quote volume for every supported swap form. | Wrong order, direction, exactness, net volume, partial fill, reentrant self-call, or foreign callback changes liabilities. | All eight forms use gross volume, exact output gross-up is monotone, partial fills revert whole swaps, and same-pool self-calls are forbidden. |
| TB-P3-06 fee-ledger-to-beneficiary-claims | Independent 10, 40, and 250 basis-point liabilities become claims only through their assigned identity and destination rules. | A claim redirects a beneficiary, changes remainders, or spends an unfunded liability. | Claims preserve lifetime remainders, require nonzero allowed destinations, and leave the hook solvent. |
| TB-P3-07 process-liability-to-Operations | Current Operations claims a positive, bounded process amount for one permanently unique cycle. | An unrelated caller, alternate destination, reuse, expired-window miscount, capacity reset, an entry count above 64, or stale rotation releases value. | `claimProcess` requires self destination, strict less-than-21,600-second accounting, `processClaimMaxCount <= 64`, immutable 500000 USDG `Xmax`, and permanent history. |
| TB-P3-08 Treasury-to-claim-controls | Treasury changes limits, pause state, and emergency rotation within the narrow control surface. | A limit increase becomes immediate, clears usage, an Operations caller cancels a pending rotation, or an ordinary handover resets its delay. | Decreases and zero apply immediately; increases wait six hours; the rotation delay is immutable at 43200 seconds; only a new Treasury intent may replace a pending rotation; schedule and rotation auto-pause; history never resets. |
| TB-P3-09 Operations-identities-to-signer | The Operations EVM and Operations Solana identities sign pre-validated actions outside Node. | A third identity, process private key, unrecorded exception, or opaque message is signed. | Exactly two identities exist; signer access is external unless a recorded owner exception applies. |
| TB-P3-10 provider-output-to-transaction-policy | Provider routes and transactions are untrusted input to semantic decoding. | An unexpected chain, program, selector, source, destination, mint, amount, fee, co-signer, instruction, deadline, blockhash, or noncanonical schema is accepted. | The runner's `hookemon.transaction-policy.v1` version 1 validator is authoritative; adapters emit the identical schema; decode, allowlist, then revalidate before broadcast. |
| TB-P3-11 repository-to-irreversible-effect | One CycleRepository journals every money mutation, immutable cycle mode, custody observation, durable attempt, Relay leg, standing-authority decision, and wallet nonce reservation before the effect. | A UI, scheduler, adapter, retry, stale worker, or cross-mode service creates an alternate state, a duplicate effect, a second nonce reservation, or a duplicate standing-authority cap reservation. | Provider attempts distinguish retryable `NOT_SENT` from observation-only `SENT_UNKNOWN`; chain attempts bind policy and fencing digests; a wallet-wide nonce and fencing lock covers claim, outbound, return, and payout; authority first use checks wall-clock expiry and atomically records its cap and nonce reservations; replay is idempotent. |
| TB-P3-12 cycle-stages-to-return | A single cycle progresses from eligibility snapshot through the prescribed stages, spends only its claimed principal outbound, and returns only its attributable configured-Solana-stablecoin proceeds delta. | Cross-cycle funding, an API response, a quote, an unfinalized balance, a double-counted unresolved principal, an unrelated inbound asset, a partial relay finality, or a wrong-asset destination credit counts as proceeds. | Every `RelayLegV1` has globally unique source and destination hashes. This process observes both finalized deltas through its own RPC clients and attributes the destination by amount, time window, and memo or request identifier. Partial, refund, late, and wrong-asset outcomes enter named `HELD_RELAY_*` terminal states; completion requires a finalized attributed USDG credit. |
| TB-P3-13 epic-provider-data-to-sale-policy | Insured value and offer data determine sell-or-hold at the epic gate. | Equality is held, unsupported tier or `contains` data advances, unreconciled insured value sells, a changed quote sells, or held inventory silently clears. | Hold iff `offerAtomic * 100 < insuredValueAtomic * 40`; Collector validation requires numeric tiers 1 through 4, accepted `contains`, idempotent open, and instant-buyback reconciliation; failures enter `HELD_*`; v1 holds block new claims. |
| TB-P3-14 chain-history-to-eligibility | Finalized transfer history establishes holders before process claim. | A post-claim snapshot, reorg, missing logs, unverified archive response, or mismatched launch supply changes eligibility. | Pin and recheck block hash, replay from deployment, reconcile mint minus burn, and prove log completeness independently. |
| TB-P3-15 payout-manifest-to-recipient-transfer | The frozen manifest turns a finalized attributed USDG return into direct per-holder transfers. | An infeasible payout starts, manifest bytes mutate, a restart loses `SIGNED`, a retry alters nonce, blockhash, or calldata, a frozen recipient stops the manifest, dust loses provenance, or frozen recipients lose their liability. | Feasibility precedes claim; first broadcast makes bytes immutable; signed records remain durable; receipt search or same-nonce-and-calldata replacement resolves a dropped broadcast; frozen recipients are quarantined without blocking the manifest; dust carry is atomically persisted and consumed with cycle provenance. |
| TB-P3-16 policy-and-canary-to-live-readiness | Policy caps, pause, manual approvals, canaries, and graph evidence feed `launchEligible`. | A code-only repair, stale provider fact, ignored pause, untested signer, or missing gas reserve is reported as launch ready. | `repairMergeEligible` and `launchEligible` remain distinct; the policy engine checks every signature boundary. |
