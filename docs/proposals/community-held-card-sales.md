# Community-informed held-card sales

The owner specified on September 5, 2026 that each held card receives community consultation before the owner chooses Collector Crypt buyback or a separate marketplace sale. Community feedback informs the decision; no binding vote or token governance mechanism was specified.

The requested extension resumes automatic holder payments after actual sale proceeds are received and verified for the original cycle. It preserves the frozen eligibility snapshot, payment finality requirements, duplicate-payment protection and held-cycle accounting. A sale agreement or a marketplace listing is not settlement evidence.

The current standard buyback path does not establish support for arbitrary marketplace settlements. Before implementation, bind the selected marketplace, custody and transfer authorization, settlement asset, fees, proof linking the sale to the held NFT, and the conversion and return into the original cycle. Never treat an unrelated deposit as card sale proceeds. Existing HELD controls remain in force until an explicit supported recovery transition is implemented.

This proposal records the requested extension to REQ-epic-gate-1 and REQ-direct-payout-1. It does not change the authoritative requirements or enable a money-moving path.
