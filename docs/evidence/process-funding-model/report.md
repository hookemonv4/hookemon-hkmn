Let p be the actual all-in USD acquisition cost per USDG, q the USD cost per ETH, E the eventual bounded ETH requirement covering deployment, approvals, seed, separate swaps, claim, bridge and return, and C all other new-owner funding costs in USD (including Solana gas/rent and acquisition charges not already included in p/q). The required condition is 150*p + E*q + C <= 250. Therefore p_max=(250-E*q-C)/150 and q_max=(250-150*p-C)/E when E>0. Even with zero other costs p cannot exceed 1.6666666667 USD/USDG; that is only an absolute mathematical ceiling, not an executable acquisition allowance. There is no numeric reliable ETH/USD threshold until E and C are bounded. The quote input USD estimate 25.296671/25.298644=0.9999220116 is not a USDG acquisition quote and is not substituted for p.

A complete <=250 USD test remains conditional on priced acquisition, launch/seed feasibility for actual tokens, bounded gas for separate production transactions, deployment and Solana expenses, and live provider bindings.
