# Phase 1 Continuous-Work Autonomy Grant

## Owner authorization

On 30 August 2026, the owner instructed the orchestrator to keep working continuously, treat ordinary in-scope decisions as pre-approved, and continue on already available work whenever a separate approval would otherwise pause progress.

## Authorized target

Continue through the approved Product Phase 1 task ledger and non-live release preparation without stopping for reversible, repository-local decisions that remain within Requirements Revision 54, Architecture Revision 3, the approved F2 survivability bounds, and the existing task cards.

The grant includes:

- autonomous phase chaining and execution of the next eligible ledger task;
- isolated branches and worktrees from current `origin/main`;
- test-first implementation, reviews, commits, pushes, and Draft PRs;
- routine technical choices that preserve frozen interfaces and approved requirements; and
- parallel execution only across disjoint R6 write sets.

## Hard stops and exclusions

This grant does not authorize:

- a merge to `main` other than PR #1, whose merge the owner separately approved;
- deployment, submission, signing, broadcast, publication, or another onchain action;
- spending, money movement, secret access, credential use, or wallet access;
- a gate override, `NOT_APPLICABLE` classification, scope expansion, or requirements change;
- inventing an unresolved provider, address, route, cap, deployment, or production fact; or
- using or referencing any repository other than `https://github.com/hookemonv4/hkmn.git`.

When a hard stop is reached, work continues on every other eligible disjoint task. The orchestrator bundles exact pending actions for the owner instead of interrupting routine progress.
