# P1-002 — Execute canonical market swap

## TASK

Implement the smallest executable canonical-market slice that authenticates one frozen USDG/HKMN PoolKey, derives actual finalized USDG execution across every supported swap mode, and preserves the buyer's exact canonical HKMN output.

## RELEVANT FILES

- `architecture/interfaces.json` — Frozen provider and callback contract from `P1-001`; consume without unilateral changes.
- `packages/contracts/src/market/CanonicalMarket.sol` — New canonical PoolKey, callback, delta, fee-route, and custody validation component.
- `packages/contracts/src/market/PermanentLaunchPosition.sol` — New provider-compatible immutable custody construction if the frozen interface requires a dedicated component.
- `packages/contracts/test/market/CanonicalMarket.t.sol` — Eight-quadrant swap, authentication, rounding, rollback, custody, and post-custody trading tests.
- `docs/modules/canonical-market.md` — Canonical-market contract card that must match the implemented behavior.
- `docs/modules/token-core.md` — Issuance boundary consumed by the permanent market allocation.

## PATTERN TO FOLLOW

Follow `docs/modules/canonical-market.md`, `decisions/ADR-0016-exact-fee-rounding.md`, `decisions/ADR-0017-permanent-zero-lp-fee-market.md`, and the exact frozen interfaces from `P1-001`. Use final balance and delta evidence, never displayed token order or nominal input.

## CONSTRAINT

The canonical PoolKey static LP fee is exactly zero. The approved inclusive 3.00 percent Hookemon hook fee is the only trading fee on the canonical route; no protocol, router, provider, integrator, token-transfer, or other surcharge may supplement it, while network gas remains separate. No worker, deployer, treasury, Operations account, or other project identity may transfer, approve, decrease, withdraw, collect fees from, rescue, upgrade, delegate, or succeed to the launch position. Unsupported partial execution reverts completely. Do not modify shared frozen interfaces without a spec-sync stop.

## ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

- `REQ-canonical-market-1` and `REQ-canonical-market-2`: only the frozen PoolManager and complete canonical PoolKey reach executed-volume output; foreign pools, malformed callbacks, wrong stages, reentrancy, zero output, and unsupported partial fills revert without liability mutation.
- `REQ-canonical-market-3`: exact-input and exact-output buys and sells across both address orderings derive USDG and HKMN solely from final token addresses and deltas, never display order or nominal input.
- `REQ-canonical-market-4`: every accepted buy preserves the buyer's exact canonical HKMN output while the already frozen inclusive fee and permanent-custody contract remains true for supported post-custody trading.
