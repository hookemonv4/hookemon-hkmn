# P2-002 — Isolate peg cycle custody

## TASK

Give every sequential cycle its own deterministic immutable return escrow while retaining one hook-bound coordinator and one active cycle.

## RELEVANT FILES

- `packages/contracts/src/process/PegCycleReturnEscrow.sol` — Cycle-specific exact-destination custody runtime.
- `packages/contracts/src/process/IPegCycleVault.sol` — Coordinator authorization, lifecycle, history, cleanup, and renewal interface.
- `packages/contracts/src/process/PegCycleVault.sol` — Single-active-cycle coordinator and deterministic escrow binding.
- `packages/contracts/src/process/ProcessBudget.sol` — Exact process-liability funding into the computed escrow.
- `packages/contracts/test/process/PegCycleVaultRecovery.t.sol` — Failure successor, replay, cleanup, renewal, and delayed-return isolation tests.
- `packages/contracts/test/process/PegCycleVault.t.sol` — Core funding, outbound, return, and payout state-machine tests.
- `packages/contracts/test/process/ProcessBudget.t.sol` — Exact funding and zero-Operations-custody tests.
- `packages/contracts/test/payout/PayoutCommitment.t.sol` — Cycle-escrow payout commitment integration.
- `packages/contracts/test/invariant/PhaseOneReleaseInvariant.t.sol` — Stateful custody and conservation coverage.
- `docs/modules/peg-cycle-vault.md` — Coordinator and escrow contract card.
- `docs/modules/process-budget.md` — Funding contract card.

## PATTERN TO FOLLOW

Follow `ADR-0020`, `architecture/interfaces.json`, and the exact-transfer and replay patterns in the Phase 1 money path. Derive the escrow with `CREATE2` from the cycle identifier and immutable constructor identities.

## CONSTRAINT

The coordinator has no generic call, approval, recipient, rescue, sweep, upgrade, proxy, owner, or successor surface. Operations remains a trigger with zero principal custody. A terminal cycle, identifier, nonce, escrow, or receipt is never reused.

## DEPENDENCIES

- `P2-001` Bind lean manual cycle control.

## REQUIREMENTS

- `REQ-cycle-control-1` at requirements revision 57.

## ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

- Funding, outbound execution, attributable return, and payout use only the active cycle's computed escrow; distinct cycle identifiers produce distinct immutable escrows.
- An evidenced `FAILED` cycle permits only a fresh successor bound to the exact predecessor and failure receipt, while delayed funds remain in the predecessor escrow.
- Pending cleanup preserves consumed identities, exact-subject deadline renewal changes only expiry and a fresh nonce, and all replay or altered-field attempts leave state and balances unchanged.
