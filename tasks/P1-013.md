## PATTERN TO FOLLOW

Follow the frozen canonical-market and fee-accounting interfaces. Keep PoolManager callback authentication, executed USDG volume, collected fee value, and immutable beneficiary liabilities distinct.

## CONSTRAINT

Direct hook and vault USDG transfers require canonical return data and exact balance movement; PoolManager-mediated collection is accepted only from the exact observed hook balance increase. The hook and vault must share one immutable USDG identity. Do not treat this composition as permanent-custody or deployed-provider evidence.
