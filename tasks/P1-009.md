# P1-009 — Compile realized holder distribution

## TASK

Build a deterministic distribution compiler that reconciles one closed cycle to actual returned USDG, validates one owner-approved direct-holder manifest, and emits the canonical fixed-depth Merkle-sum payout artifacts.

## RELEVANT FILES

- `architecture/interfaces.json` — Frozen cycle, payout, holder snapshot, and proof schemas from `P1-001`.
- `packages/runner/src/distribution/reconcile.mjs` — New exact cycle-ledger and attributable-return reconciliation.
- `packages/runner/src/distribution/manifest.mjs` — New canonical recipient, index, amount, and digest compiler.
- `packages/runner/src/distribution/merkle-sum.mjs` — New depth-10 Merkle-sum tree and proof implementation.
- `packages/runner/test/distribution/` — New deterministic fixtures, edge vectors, reconstruction tests, and independent reference comparisons.
- `docs/modules/cycle-runner.md` — Cycle-runner contract card for distribution preparation and payout handoff.
- `docs/modules/payout-commitment.md` — Payout proof-domain contract card consumed by the compiler.

## PATTERN TO FOLLOW

Follow `docs/modules/cycle-runner.md`, `docs/modules/payout-commitment.md`, `decisions/ADR-0014-sum-bound-permissionless-payouts.md`, and the proof interface frozen by `P1-001`. Build from finalized balance and custody deltas, not price, insured value, quotes, API status, or estimated conversion.

## CONSTRAINT

The compiler is deterministic and offline. It never signs, broadcasts, funds a payout, chooses recipients, infers ownership policy, or calls a marketplace. Inputs must bind one closed activity-isolated cycle, one finalized Robinhood snapshot block, and one explicit owner-approved manifest. Every recipient is nonzero and unique, every index is unique in `0..1023`, every amount is positive, and the root sum equals the exact attributable returned USDG credit.

## ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

- `REQ-cycle-runner-2`: reconciliation proves the ordered USDG, USD Coin, pack, NFT, buyback, return-route, gas, and final Operations balance deltas; every unresolved, unrelated, short, wrong-asset, nonfinal, or mismatched input yields no payout artifact.
- `REQ-holder-settlement-5`: canonical bytes, leaf domain, node domain, depth, indices, sums, root, manifest digest, and proofs reproduce byte-for-byte from either independent publication copy and the release archive.
- `REQ-cycle-runner-2`: finalized direct HKMN balances, unique nonzero recipients, unique indices, positive amounts, and exact sum equality are tested against duplicates, nonholders, zero fields, malformed manifests, wrong snapshot blocks, and every boundary size through 1,024 entries.
