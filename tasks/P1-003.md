# P1-003 — Issue fixed HKMN supply

## TASK

Implement one provider-bound launch path that creates the exact fixed HKMN supply once, assigns 90 percent only through the canonical permanent market path, and applies the approved evidence-backed treatment to the remaining 10 percent.

## RELEVANT FILES

- `architecture/interfaces.json` — Frozen launcher, token, market, and custody interfaces consumed from `P1-001` and `P1-002`.
- `packages/contracts/src/launch/HookemonLaunchStrategy.sol` — New custom launch strategy or compatible migration component selected by the frozen provider evidence.
- `packages/contracts/src/launch/HookemonIssuance.sol` — New single-use issuance and allocation validation component where required by the official mechanism.
- `packages/contracts/test/launch/HookemonIssuance.t.sol` — Supply, decimals, allocation, immutability, transfer, and duplicate-launch tests.
- `docs/modules/token-core.md` — Token-core contract card that must match the implemented launch behavior.

## PATTERN TO FOLLOW

Follow `docs/modules/token-core.md`, `decisions/ADR-0002-launchpad-token-issuance.md`, the provider interface frozen by `P1-001`, and the market custody contract frozen by `P1-002`. Use the official UERC20Factory and launcher semantics only where exact source and runtime evidence proves compatibility.

## CONSTRAINT

The supply is exactly 420,690,000,000 HKMN adjusted for the officially bound decimals and can be created once. The token exposes no mint, blacklist, confiscation, hidden transfer tax, proxy upgrade, delegatecall replacement, or destructive project control. The 90 percent allocation never passes through a freely controlled project wallet. The 10 percent branch must be one officially evidenced approved lock, vesting, or non-circulating undistributed state; an unresolved provider capability blocks completion.

## ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

- `REQ-token-core-1`: deployment tests prove exact name, symbol, decimals, total supply, one-time issuance, 90 percent canonical-market allocation, exact 10 percent approved treatment, zero other allocations, and rejection of duplicate or unauthorized creation.
- `REQ-token-core-2` and `REQ-token-core-3`: ABI, selector, opcode, authorization, fuzz, zero-value, self-transfer, and maximum-practical transfer tests prove no privileged token controls or project fee on wallet transfers.
- `REQ-token-core-4`: the exact official launcher ABI, source revision, runtime hash, decimals behavior, allocation behavior, and selected 10 percent capability are frozen and executable with no unresolved field.
