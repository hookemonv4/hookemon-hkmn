# P1-006 — Release one process cycle

## TASK

Implement one complete process-budget opening that moves an exact positive amount of accrued process USDG from the hook only to its immutable mutually bound `PegCycleVault`, without adding hook policy state or Operations custody.

## RELEVANT FILES

- `architecture/interfaces.json` — Frozen shared money-path interfaces from the level-0 task; consume without modification.
- `packages/contracts/src/accounting/FeeAccounting.sol` — Process-liability debit and global money-path lock delivered by `P1-004`; consume without modification.
- `packages/contracts/src/access/MoneyRoles.sol` — Current and historical Operations authority delivered by `P1-005`; consume without modification.
- `packages/contracts/src/process/ProcessBudget.sol` — New atomic vault-only opening, immutable cycle record, and replay-protection component.
- `packages/contracts/src/process/PegCycleVault.sol` — New immutable one-cycle custody state machine mutually bound to the hook.
- `packages/contracts/test/process/ProcessBudget.t.sol` — New boundary, authorization, replay, token-failure, reentrancy, and operation-isolation tests.
- `docs/modules/process-budget.md` — Process-budget contract card that must describe the implemented interface, invariants, commands, and recovery path.

## PATTERN TO FOLLOW

Follow `docs/modules/process-budget.md`, `decisions/ADR-0019-immutable-peg-cycle-custody.md`, `architecture/trust-boundaries.md` boundary `TB-08` and `TB-09`, and the frozen interfaces produced by `P1-004` and `P1-005`. Treat exact pre-call and post-call USDG balance deltas as the transfer result. The runner task owns owner authorization and spend-limit preflight.

## CONSTRAINT

`P1-004` and `P1-005` must be complete before this task starts. Do not edit their source or test paths. Only the current Operations account may trigger an opening; the destination is the mutually bound immutable vault, and a successful opening permanently binds `{cycleId, amount, vault, operationsTrigger}`. Operations never receives process principal. The immutable authorizer must be distinct from Operations, treasury, Programmable, hook, and vault. The hook and vault must expose no admin, pause, mutable cap, policy registry, specification hash, scheduler, marketplace, pack configuration, arbitrary destination, proxy, upgrade, successor-control, generic call, approval, rescue, sweep, delegatecall, arbitrary transfer, or arbitrary external-execution function. The exact immutable typed route executor is required; generic targets, selectors, calls, and approvals remain forbidden. Do not sign, broadcast, purchase, spend, or access secrets.

## ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

- `REQ-process-budget-1` and `REQ-process-budget-2`: zero, equality, plus-one, trigger, vault-only destination, replay, handover, false-return, revert, malformed-return, short-delta, excess-delta, and reentrancy tests prove that transfer, process-liability debit, replay mark, and immutable vault-cycle record either commit together for the exact amount or leave every balance and state field unchanged.
- `REQ-process-budget-3`: the supported-operation matrix proves that rejected and unavailable releases do not change unused process liability or block canonical trading, fee accrual, fee claims, valid payout funding, or already funded entitlement payments.
- `REQ-process-budget-5`: ABI, selector, storage, and event scans find no speculative process-policy, mutable cap, scheduler, marketplace, arbitrary-destination, or arbitrary external-execution surface.
- Immutable-authorizer and forbidden-vault-surface tests prove that the authorizer is distinct from Operations, treasury, Programmable, hook, and vault, and that no proxy, upgrade, successor-control, generic call, approval, rescue, sweep, delegatecall, or arbitrary transfer exists.
