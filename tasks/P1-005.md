# P1-005 — Enforce minimal money roles

## TASK

Implement the minimal caller matrix plus two-step Treasury and Operations-trigger successor handovers while preserving historical beneficiary rights, immutable cycle metadata, and vault custody.

## RELEVANT FILES

- `architecture/interfaces.json` — Frozen initialization identities and money-operation surface from `P1-001`.
- `packages/contracts/src/access/MoneyRoles.sol` — New minimal role and two-step handover component.
- `packages/contracts/test/access/MoneyRoles.t.sol` — Caller matrix, handover, stale proposal, historical-right, and forbidden-role tests.
- `docs/modules/role-control.md` — Role-control contract card that must match the implemented authority surface.

## PATTERN TO FOLLOW

Follow `docs/modules/role-control.md`, `decisions/ADR-0011-operations-wallet-cycle-authority.md`, and `decisions/ADR-0015-minimal-money-authority.md`. Model authority per operation, never through a generic owner or administrator.

## CONSTRAINT

Treasury and Operations are exact nonzero owner-approved role identities but the hook must not assume a wallet implementation. Operations is trigger and audit metadata only: it never receives process principal or returned proceeds and never acts as payout funder. Only the current account may propose its nonzero successor, and only that exact successor may accept. No generic admin, automation, pause, fee-policy, recipient-replacement, position-control, or upgrade role may exist. A handover never rewrites an accrued beneficiary, cycle-bound Operations trigger, immutable PegCycleVault, authorizer, typed route executor, or vault-funded payout authorization.

## ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

- `REQ-role-control-1`: exhaustive caller-by-operation tests prove that every money mutation accepts only its named role or permissionless caller; current Operations can trigger only exact-authorized hook-to-PegCycleVault funding, while any caller may submit the exact vault-funded payout authorization, and every cross-role, zero, arbitrary, redirected, or contract-internal escalation path is rejected.
- `REQ-role-control-2`: two-step Treasury and Operations-trigger tests cover proposal, exact acceptance, replacement, cancellation behavior if specified, zero or stale candidates, and repeated attempts without requiring a particular account type; handover preserves cycle trigger metadata, immutable vault custody, and permissionless payout funding.
- `REQ-role-control-3` and `REQ-role-control-4`: ABI, storage, selector, and sequence tests prove no admin, automation, pause, economics, historical-right rewrite, user-freeze, launch-position control, or upgrade authority.
