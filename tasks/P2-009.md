# P2-009 — Preserve cycle progress with quarantined surplus

## TASK

Keep exact authorized outbound and payout progress live when unsolicited USDG arrives in the same cycle escrow, without attributing or moving that surplus.

## RELEVANT FILES

- `packages/contracts/src/process/PegCycleVault.sol` — Exact principal and root-sum transfers that preserve cycle-local surplus.
- `packages/contracts/src/process/IPegCycleVault.sol` — Historical lifecycle read interface.
- `packages/contracts/test/process/PegCycleVault.t.sol` — Surplus liveness, lifecycle, and active-read vectors.
- `packages/contracts/test/process/CompileOnlyPegCycleVault.sol` — Interface-compatible test stubs.
- `packages/contracts/test/process/PegCycleVault.surface.test.mjs` — Exact ABI allowlist and runtime-size check.
- `docs/modules/peg-cycle-vault.md` — Surplus quarantine and lifecycle contract card.

## PATTERN TO FOLLOW

Follow the exact-delta money-path checks in `PegCycleVault` and the cycle-isolation rule in `ADR-0020`. Compare post-transfer balance to pre-transfer balance minus the authorized amount; never infer authority from the surplus.

## CONSTRAINT

Initial funding still requires an empty escrow and one exact authorized funding delta. Surplus cannot increase principal, returned proceeds, payout root, or successor credit and exposes no rescue or withdrawal surface.

## DEPENDENCIES

- `P2-002` Isolate peg cycle custody.

## REQUIREMENTS

- `REQ-cycle-control-1` at requirements revision 57.

## ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

- Surplus received after `FUNDED` cannot block outbound: exactly the authorized principal leaves and all surplus remains in that cycle escrow.
- Payout authorization and commitment require at least the evidenced root sum, move exactly that root sum to the hook, and leave earlier or later surplus in the original escrow.
- Active authorization reads are populated only for `FUNDED`, `OUTBOUND`, and `RETURNED`; cycle-keyed history stays readable, the public ABI matches its allowlist, and runtime remains below 24,576 bytes.
