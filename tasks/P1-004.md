# P1-004 — Accrue isolated USDG liabilities

## TASK

Implement exact USDG-denominated accrual, beneficiary freezing, isolated claims, and global solvency for the inclusive Hookemon fee generated by one authenticated canonical swap.

## RELEVANT FILES

- `architecture/interfaces.json` — Frozen executed-USDG observation and token behavior consumed from `P1-001` and `P1-002`.
- `packages/contracts/src/accounting/FeeAccounting.sol` — New liability split, accrual, claim, and solvency component.
- `packages/contracts/test/accounting/FeeAccounting.t.sol` — Rounding, claims, token-failure, reentrancy, dust, and aggregate-solvency tests.
- `docs/modules/fee-accounting.md` — Fee-accounting contract card that must match the implemented public surface and invariants.

## PATTERN TO FOLLOW

Follow `docs/modules/fee-accounting.md`, `decisions/ADR-0003-segregated-usdg-liabilities.md`, and `decisions/ADR-0016-exact-fee-rounding.md`. Consume only the finalized executed-USDG observation frozen by `P1-002` and prove every state mutation with actual USDG balance deltas.

## CONSTRAINT

Calculate `total = floor(Q * 300 / 10_000)`, `programmable = floor(Q * 10 / 10_000)`, `treasury = floor(Q * 40 / 10_000)`, and `process = total - programmable - treasury`. Beneficiaries freeze at accrual. A failed claim changes no liability or unrelated balance. There is no surplus withdrawal, arbitrary recipient, hidden fee, or second USDG custody address in V1.

## ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

- `REQ-fee-accounting-2`: boundary, minimum-unit, maximum-practical, and fuzz tests consume the frozen revision-54 split and prove immutable beneficiary ownership for every authenticated executed-USDG accrual.
- `REQ-fee-accounting-3` and `REQ-fee-accounting-5`: Programmable and treasury claims transfer only their exact positive liabilities, isolate failures, reject reentrancy and false-return or short-delta tokens, and never affect process or payout liabilities.
- `REQ-fee-accounting-4`: after every accrual, claim, release, funding, payment, revert, and adversarial sequence, actual hook USDG balance is at least the sum of every outstanding liability with no invented or double-counted value.
