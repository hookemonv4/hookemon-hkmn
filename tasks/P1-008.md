# P1-008 — Pay one holder entitlement

## TASK

Implement the single permissionless settlement path that verifies one funded payout proof, transfers the exact USDG amount only to the committed recipient, marks the payout-and-index paid once, and leaves every unrelated entitlement independent.

## RELEVANT FILES

- `architecture/interfaces.json` — Frozen shared payout, accounting, token, and settlement interfaces; consume without modification.
- `packages/contracts/src/payout/CanonicalMerkleSum.sol` — Canonical proof verifier delivered by `P1-007`; consume without modification.
- `packages/contracts/src/payout/PayoutCommitment.sol` — Immutable payout identity and unpaid-liability interface delivered by `P1-007`; consume without modification.
- `packages/contracts/src/settlement/HolderSettlement.sol` — New permissionless one-entitlement payment, paid-key, exact-delta, and replay-protection component.
- `packages/contracts/test/settlement/HolderSettlement.t.sol` — New all-index, caller, proof, replay, recipient-failure, long-horizon, reentrancy, solvency, and gas-bound tests.
- `docs/modules/holder-settlement.md` — Holder-settlement contract card that must describe the implemented interface, invariants, commands, and recovery path.

## PATTERN TO FOLLOW

Follow `docs/modules/holder-settlement.md`, `decisions/ADR-0014-sum-bound-permissionless-payouts.md`, and `architecture/trust-boundaries.md` boundary `TB-17`. Consume the exact proof domain frozen by `P1-007`, derive the paid key only from `{payoutId, index}`, keep the committed recipient independent of the caller, and validate success through exact hook and recipient USDG balance deltas.

## CONSTRAINT

`P1-007` must be complete before this task starts, and its source, tests, vectors, and payout metadata are read-only here. Any caller may submit a proof, but no caller or role may choose, replace, remap, or redirect the recipient. V1 has one immediate non-expiring per-entitlement function with no batch, worker-only period, readiness delay, emergency mode, explicit failure state, administrator, automation role, or recovery selector. A failed token call or proof reverts the complete leaf transition under the shared money-path lock.

## ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

- `REQ-holder-settlement-1`, `REQ-holder-settlement-2`, and `REQ-holder-settlement-3`: every index, arbitrary caller, immediate call, long-horizon call, retry, duplicate, altered leaf, malformed path, reordered proof, and isolated recipient-failure vector proves at most one exact transfer to the committed recipient and no blocking or mutation of a distinct leaf.
- `REQ-holder-settlement-4`: ABI, selector, event, storage, and adversarial-call scans find no recipient replacement, remapping, alternate payout, administrator, or recovery surface.
- `REQ-holder-settlement-6` and `REQ-holder-settlement-7`: index-zero, index-1,023, first-payment, replay, malformed-depth, maximum-sum, token-failure, proxy-behavior, exact-delta, and reentrancy tests record positive gas headroom against the frozen Robinhood limits and produce zero underpayment, double payment, surviving partial state, or solvency violation.
