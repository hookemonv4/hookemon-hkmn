Each rarity has its own story.

Common, Uncommon, Rare and Epic are the provider’s pack tiers. They describe that pack’s value bands, rather than the printed rarity of a Pokémon card. A card’s tier can differ between packs. The library shows individual cards currently reported by the provider; a list of cards does not imply equal draw probabilities.
