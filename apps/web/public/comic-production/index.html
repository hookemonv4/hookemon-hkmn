05 / Quick answers

Common questions

Read the holder guide →
What is Hookemon?+

Hookemon buys gacha card packs, opens them, sells the cards and pays the proceeds to HKMN holders. Trading fees fund the packs.

Do I receive the cards myself?+

The cards are part of the shared cycle. Hookemon opens the packs and sells the cards; proceeds can then be distributed to eligible HKMN holders. Holding HKMN does not assign a specific card to you.

When does the next cycle begin?+

The dashboard shows the next cycle time when a verified schedule is available. A cycle depends on available funding and the completion of its required steps. If no schedule is available, the dashboard says so.

How much will I receive?+

Your share is proportional to the eligible HKMN you hold at the cycle’s snapshot. We divide your eligible balance by the total eligible balance, then multiply that share by the finalized returned proceeds available to distribute.

For example, holding 1% of the eligible HKMN gives you 1% of that cycle’s distributable amount. If the cycle distributes 1 ETH, your share is 0.01 ETH. This is an example, not a promised payment.

Allocations round down to the smallest ETH unit (one wei); the remainder carries into a later pool. Buying or selling after the snapshot does not change that cycle’s allocation. A cycle may have no distributable proceeds. Read the holder rules →

Is every card sold immediately?+

No. The current policy holds a card if its verified buyback offer is below 40% of its verified insured value. Missing or conflicting data also pauses the sale. A held card needs an owner decision before the cycle can continue, and new cycles stay paused in the meantime.

Who decides what happens to a held card?+

We discuss each held card with the community. The project owner then decides whether to accept Collector Crypt’s offer or sell on another marketplace.

The planned flow resumes automatic payouts once the sale proceeds have arrived and are verified for that cycle. Support for sales on other marketplaces is still being built. The cycle keeps its original holder snapshot.

Are the cards shown here real pulls?+

The cards in the illustrated loop and collection section are examples from Collector Crypt inventory. Verified Hookemon pulls appear separately in the dashboard when cycle data is available.

How are the packs funded?+

The trading fee is 3,00 % auf Brutto‑Volumen: 0,10 % for Programmable, 0,40 % for Treasury and 2,50 % for card packs. Available trading fees determine the pack budget. Only finalized proceeds returned from a cycle can fund its holder payments.
