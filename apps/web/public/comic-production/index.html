Read the full cycle +
  1. Fees fund the adventure. Eligible trades contribute a 3,00 % auf Brutto‑Volumen fee. The 2,50 % pack allocation funds card packs.
  2. A pack is opened. Available funding is used to buy gacha card packs, which are then opened.
  3. The cards are revealed. Each reveal enters the cycle history. The cards in this illustration are examples.
  4. The cards are sold. Sale proceeds return to the cycle. Actual proceeds depend on the cards and available sale terms.
  5. Proceeds reach eligible holders. After costs and reserves, available proceeds can be distributed to eligible HKMN holders. Amounts and timing vary.

05 / Quick answers

A few things
before you go.

Get to know the loop.

What is Hookemon?+

Hookemon buys gacha card packs, opens them, sells the cards and pays the proceeds to HKMN holders. Trading fees fund the packs.

Do I receive the cards myself?+

The cards are part of the shared cycle. Hookemon opens the packs and sells the cards; proceeds can then be distributed to eligible HKMN holders. Holding HKMN does not assign a specific card to you.

When does the next cycle begin?+

The dashboard shows the next cycle time when a verified schedule is available. A cycle depends on available funding and the completion of its required steps. If no schedule is available, the dashboard says so.

How much do holders receive?+

Distributions depend on actual card sale proceeds, costs, reserves and holder eligibility. Amounts can vary, and a cycle may have no distributable proceeds. The dashboard shows the latest cycle's payout and average per actual recipient only when its distribution is complete. Earlier payouts are included in the total when the verified history is complete.

Are the cards shown here real pulls?+

The cards in the illustrated loop and collection section are examples from Collector Crypt inventory. Verified Hookemon pulls appear separately in the dashboard when cycle data is available.

How are the packs funded?+

The trading fee is 3,00 % auf Brutto‑Volumen: 0,10 % for Programmable, 0,40 % for Treasury and 2,50 % for card packs. Available trading fees determine the pack budget. Card sale proceeds, operating costs and reserves determine what can be distributed to eligible holders.
