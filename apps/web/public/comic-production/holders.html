Clear holder rules.

Understand the documented snapshot and distribution policy before relying on a cycle. This page does not determine your wallet’s eligibility.

The idea

Cards are sold.
Holders follow the proceeds.

Hookemon buys gacha card packs, opens them, sells the cards and pays the proceeds to HKMN holders. Trading fees fund the packs.

You do not receive ownership of an individual card or a right to its physical delivery by holding HKMN.

The documented policy

From a snapshot to a payment.

01

A frozen balance

Before claim processing, a verified snapshot records HKMN balances at a selected finalized block. Later balance changes do not rewrite that cycle’s frozen snapshot.

02

An eligible share

Positive balances count unless excluded by the launch manifest and role history. The share is proportional to the wallet’s eligible HKMN balance within the total eligible balance.

03

A recorded transfer

The current documented payout path uses direct USDG transfers. A prepared allocation is only marked paid after its transfer is finalized.

The questions that matter.

Do I need to hold for a certain time?+

The current documented policy uses the balance at the frozen snapshot block. It does not rank a top group of holders or apply a time-weighted holding score.

The live snapshot block and your wallet’s inclusion are not exposed by this page. Do not infer eligibility from a countdown alone.

What if I buy or sell after the snapshot?+

That cycle keeps the balances in its frozen snapshot. A subsequent transfer changes the balances available for a later snapshot; it does not transfer or recalculate the previous allocation.

How is an allocation calculated?+

Distributable amount × your eligible snapshot balance ÷ total eligible snapshot balance.

Allocations round down to whole atomic units. The remaining fraction is recorded and carried into a later distributable pool. Sale proceeds, costs and reserves can vary; holding HKMN does not promise a fixed payment.

Do I need to claim on this website?+

This website has no claim or signing action. The documented system prepares direct transfers to the eligible addresses. Its actual availability and each payment’s completion must be confirmed by the cycle record.

Can a cycle or a payment be held?+

Yes. Missing or conflicting data, operating limits and transfer restrictions can hold processing. The holder list must not be shortened just to fit a limit. A refused or pending payment is not presented as completed.

Policy is not wallet-specific confirmation. A personal eligibility checker requires published snapshot evidence. Until that exists, this page makes no claim about a particular wallet or its next payment. See the available cycle evidence ↗
