What the labels mean.

Insurance value, market value and sale proceeds+

An insured value is the source’s stated insurance valuation. A listing is an asking price. Actual proceeds are funds received from a completed sale. These amounts are not interchangeable.

Availability and rarity+

Pack membership and rarity must come from that pack’s provider. A collectible’s series, a high value or its presence on our homepage does not establish its pull probability. Inventory can change before a purchase.

Verified data and delayed updates+

The website validates the public feed’s format, network identity and consistency. This is not an independent audit. Profile labels distinguish test observations from mainnet records, and delayed data is marked as delayed.

Addresses, custody and supporting documents+

Project addresses must be published from the deployment manifest or configuration, with their network and role. This page does not publish unverified addresses.

Custody, insurance and redemption terms belong to the actual card provider. Hookemon does not promise its own vault, insurance cover or physical redemption service to HKMN holders.
